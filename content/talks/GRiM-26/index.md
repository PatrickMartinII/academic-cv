### Abstract
The magnitude homology of hypergraphs is a recent invariant of which there have yet to be computational tools developed. In this talk we develop a correspondence which yields the desired computational tools for this theory: a Mayer-Vietoris sequence and a Künneth formula for a newly defined hypergraph product on a subcategory of hypergraphs.
